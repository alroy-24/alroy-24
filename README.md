## About

I'm Alroy — a data scientist working across **machine learning, NLP, and deep learning**.
I like taking messy, real-world data and turning it into models and visualizations that
actually answer a question. Most of my work lives in Python, and I'm always learning
something new in the deep-learning and language space.

- 🔭 Focused on applied ML, NLP, and data-driven projects
- 🌱 Currently going deeper into deep learning & transformer models
- 💬 Happy to talk about data, ML, or anything Python
- 📫 Reach me below
