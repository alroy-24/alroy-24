## 👋 About me

I'm Alroy — a data scientist who actually enjoys the messy middle of a problem: cleaning the
data nobody wants to touch, hunting down the signal, and shipping a model or dashboard that
*gets used*. I work mostly in **Python**, across **machine learning, NLP, and deep learning**.

- 🔭 Building ML / NLP projects and data dashboards
- 🌱 Currently going deep on **transformers & modern NLP**
- 🎯 I care about work that's *useful*, not just accurate
- 💬 Ask me about data, models, or a good visualization
